The Open

Two people. Two very different nights.

David is sitting at his kitchen table the night after losing the deal. Eleven weeks of calls, showings, negotiations — gone in one phone call. What's left behind isn't an asset. It's nothing. The next commission has to replace this one from scratch, the same way it always does.

Three hundred miles away, Sandra is looking at a browser tab she opened twenty minutes ago and still hasn't filled out. She's competent. She's good at this. Two recruiters already reached out to her this week. She sits there a moment longer — then closes the tab instead.

Neither one was in crisis. Neither one was out of options. Underneath both of them, the same question, wearing two different outfits:

"How much of the life you're living right now did you actually design?"

— from the opening of A Life Designed, Not Accumulated
